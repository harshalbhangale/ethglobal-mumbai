# Reach

**Who it's for:** anyone who trades on a crypto caller's word, in the X extension, the Android and iPhone keyboards,
and the web app. Their record on Arkiv is the thing they check before they trade.

**Today:** 22 callers are tracked, with their calls and scored results readable from Arkiv. Anyone can query them
(see [schema.md](schema.md)). The same records show in all four surfaces.

**No outside users yet.** This was built at ETHGlobal Mumbai, so we can't point to people who already depend on it.

**After Mumbai:** we plan to keep tracking callers and writing results, and to ship the keyboard and extension to
real users. Arkiv is the part that carries over unchanged: the record outlives our app, because it's read-only,
owned on chain, and extendable by anyone.
